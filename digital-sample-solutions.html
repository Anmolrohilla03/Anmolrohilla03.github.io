Context & Problem

Hospital sample transport remains a critical yet error-prone process. Manual tracking leads to lost samples, delayed results, and compromised patient care. The current system lacks visibility, validation, and administrative oversight.

Reflection

This project reinforced the critical importance of understanding healthcare workflows and the life-or-death nature of sample tracking. Working closely with medical professionals provided invaluable insights into their daily challenges and helped shape a solution that truly addresses their needs.

The key to successful healthcare UX is empathy - understanding that every interaction affects patient care and outcomes. This project taught me to design with both efficiency and compassion in mind.

The iterative design process, combined with regular user feedback, was crucial in creating an interface that healthcare professionals could trust and rely on in high-pressure situations. The success of this project has led to similar implementations across other hospital systems.
