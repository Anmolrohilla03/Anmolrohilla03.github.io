Smart Asset Tracking Ecosystem for Hospitals

A connected RFID-based system designed for tablets, handhelds, and dashboards

🖼️ [Hero mockup placeholder]

Project Overview

A connected hospital ecosystem built to track and manage medical equipment in real time.

Using RFID-enabled tablets and cube readers, the system automatically logs every asset's movement between rooms, validates sanitization, and reduces manual errors.

Designed for nurses, biomed teams, and admins, it brings together tablets, cube devices, and dashboards into a unified experience that improves visibility, compliance, and turnaround time.

The Challenge

Different departments used separate processes to move assets between rooms, making it impossible to know where equipment was at any moment.

A ventilator marked "in use" could actually be sitting in a soiled room waiting for cleaning, resulting in delays and manual tracking errors.

The challenge was to create an intuitive, tablet-based solution that worked seamlessly with physical RFID hardware—requiring minimal training while fitting naturally into existing hospital workflows.

Impact & Learnings

Impact:

  • • Improved asset visibility across 4 hospital floors
  • • Reduced manual tracking time by ~40%
  • • Enabled real-time alerts for misplaced or unscanned equipment

Learnings:

  • • Designing for hardware + UI synergy required deep collaboration with engineers
  • • Simplifying the UI was key for adoption in high-pressure hospital environments
  • • Consistent iconography and terminology built user trust across teams
